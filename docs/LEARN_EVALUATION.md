# Practice table: observation script

For a short session with someone who has not played Magic, or has only
played on Arena. One person at a time, on their own phone if they have one,
at `#/practice`. Twenty minutes is plenty. This is an initial usability
sample, not a measurement of learning; three to five people show where the
table confuses, not whether it teaches.

Do not lead. Read each task aloud once, then watch. If they stall for more
than a minute, note where, then say "try the hint" and note that too.

## Before

- Have they played Magic? Arena? Another card game? Write it down.
- Motion preference: leave it as their phone has it.

## Tasks

1. **Pay a changed cost.** Open "Cast it yourself" (the Centaur Courser
   exercise). Ask them to cast the creature. Watch which sources they tap
   first and whether they read the cost. Note any refusal the table gives and
   whether they understood it.
2. **Say what is left.** Once the spell is on the stack, ask: "What is in
   your mana pool now? What happens to the lands next turn?" Note the words
   they use for tapped, pool, untap.
3. **Find the next legal move.** Still on that exercise, ask: "What can you
   do now?" The right answers are pass priority, or nothing else. Note
   whether they try to cast again or play a land, and what the table said.
4. **Predict a fight.** Open "Attack into a blocker". Ask them to answer the
   prediction aloud before choosing, and to say why. Then let them play it
   out. Note whether the result surprised them.
5. **Show it on the table.** Open "Now with real cards" on any exercise. With
   real cards if any are to hand, otherwise with two pieces of paper as lands
   and one as a creature, ask them to do the payment and say the words. Note
   what they turn sideways and whether they say what the mana is for.

## After each task, note

- Hint used: yes / no. Which.
- Success: yes / with help / no.
- The specific misunderstanding, in their words if possible ("I thought the
  land was the mana", "I thought blocking taps").
- Anything they said unprompted about the screen: what they could not find,
  what they tapped by mistake.

## After the session

- Which task confused them most, and what the table said at that moment.
- One change to the table that would have helped, if any.
- Their own one-line summary of what they learned.

## Record

Keep notes in `LEARN_PROGRESS.md` under "Evaluation", one entry per person,
by date and background only, no names. Record only what was observed. If no
session has been run, the section says so.
